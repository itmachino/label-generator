Create a minimal black line-art technical illustration of this shoe showing only the LEFT shoe from a side profile view (facing right). Use super thick clean outlines only — no shading, no fills, no color, no gradients, no shadows. Show key design details like buckles, stitching, mesh patterns, and cutouts as simple line work. The illustration should be on a pure white background with no ground line, no reflections, and no text. Style: flat technical product drawing suitable for a product label.
