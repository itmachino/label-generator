How to get a published link
  1. Open your Google Sheet
  2. Go to File → Share → Publish to web
  3. Under "Link", select your sheet tab and choose Comma-separated values (.csv)
  4. Click Publish, then copy the link and paste it above

Note: anyone who has a published link can open the sheet. Only put shoe and model details in it — nothing private.
